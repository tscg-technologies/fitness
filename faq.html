FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find transparent answers regarding our class styles, beginner preparation, membership packages, and booking procedures.

FITNESS STUDIO offers six core training styles: High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT), Strength & Resistance Training, Mat Pilates, Yoga & Mobility Flows, Dance Fitness, and Multi-Planar Functional Training. In addition, we provide personal one-to-one coaching and seasonal workshops.

Yes, absolutely. Our studio was specifically created to be accessible and welcoming to individuals at any stage of their fitness journey. Every exercise format offers scalable regressions, lighter load options, and coach modifications to ensure you feel supported.

No prior gym experience is required. All classes are fully coached from start to finish. Instructors demonstrate the mechanics of each movement, monitor form throughout the workout, and suggest adaptations whenever needed.

Please wear comfortable, breathable athletic apparel and clean indoor training shoes. Bring a reusable water bottle to use with our complimentary filtered hydration stations. We provide studio workout mats, towels, and secure digital lockers.

Most studio classes range between 45 and 60 minutes. Every session includes a deliberate 5–8 minute dynamic movement warm-up, the main workout phase, and a guided 5–8 minute cool-down and recovery stretch.

Yes. We offer single-class drop-in passes for individuals who wish to experience a class before committing to multi-session packages or ongoing monthly studio access.

We provide Single Class Passes, 5-Class Packs, 10-Class Packs, Monthly Memberships (12 classes/month), Unlimited Monthly Memberships, and dedicated Personal Training Packages. Details can be viewed on our Membership Page.

If you have pre-existing cardiovascular conditions, joint injuries, pregnancy considerations, or recent surgeries, we encourage you to inform your instructor prior to class and obtain clearance from a qualified medical doctor.
