The Science of Intervals

HOW INTERVAL-BASED TRAINING WORKS

High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) pairs short, controlled bouts of elevated physical effort with deliberate recovery rest intervals.

By challenging your cardiovascular system in controlled waves rather than steady-state fatigue, interval training trains both aerobic and anaerobic energy systems efficiently. Heart rate spikes encourage muscular recruitment, while recovery windows give your muscles time to clear metabolic byproducts and prepare for the next round.

In our studio, interval durations are timed carefully with acoustic signals, allowing participants to focus solely on their breathing, form, and personal effort level.

Class Formats

OUR HIIT CLASS VARIATIONS

Tailored interval styles designed to match different preferences, joint tolerances, and experience levels.

Standard Format

Interval Conditioning

Timed 40-second work blocks followed by 20-second recovery windows utilizing ski-ergs, rowing machines, and agility cones.

Zero Equipment

Bodyweight HIIT

Explosive plyometric squats, bear crawls, mountain climbers, and tempo push-ups focusing entirely on kinetic control and body mastery.

Joint-Friendly

Low-Impact HIIT

High metabolic intensity without jarring joint stress. Employs slide boards, kettlebell deadlifts, battle ropes, and stationary bikes.

Aerobic Engine

Cardio Intervals

Sustained threshold training designed to expand lung capacity, build endurance pacing, and optimize heart rate recovery efficiency.

Foundation

Beginner HIIT

Extended recovery ratios (e.g. 20s work : 40s rest) with detailed instruction on posture, breathing rhythm, and perceived exertion scales.

Community

Group HIIT Sessions

Partner relays and synchronized pod circuits where group enthusiasm inspires you to give your best effort on every repetition.

Illustrative Workout Blueprint

SAMPLE 45-MINUTE HIIT SESSION

Here is an illustrative preview showing how our coaches structure a standard interval class from arrival to cooldown.

Phase 1 • 8 Mins

Dynamic Activation

Joint rotations, thoracic spine openers, cat-cow flows, and light skipping to raise core temperature and lubricate joints.

Phase 2 • 14 Mins

Power Circuit

4 rounds: Kettlebell swings (35s), Plyo lunges / step-backs (35s), Battle ropes (35s), followed by 60s active recovery.

Phase 3 • 15 Mins

Cardio Threshold

Tabata sprint blocks on rowing machines and agility ladders (20s sprint : 10s rest Ã— 8 rounds).

Phase 4 • 8 Mins

Recovery & Cool-Down

Down-tempo nasal breathing, hamstring stretches, hip flexor releases, and guided deceleration to lower resting heart rate.

Health & Pacing Notice

Training intensity should always be adapted to individual ability. In our HIIT classes, coaches continually emphasize that your heart rate and perceived exertion are personal to you. We do not make guaranteed calorie-burn or weight-loss claims, because physiological responses differ between individuals. Listen to your body and hydrate regularly.
