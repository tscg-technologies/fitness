Tailored Guidance

ONE-TO-ONE COACHING DEDICATED TO YOU

Personal training provides undivided attention from an experienced fitness coach focused entirely on your movement patterns.

Every individual arrives with unique anatomy, posture history, work demands, and fitness milestones. Our 1-on-1 sessions begin with an interactive movement screen to assess your hip mobility, shoulder range, core stability, and baseline strength. From there, each workout is custom programmed to build upon your progress session by session.

Whether you want to master barbell squats, rebuild confidence after time away from exercise, or elevate athletic conditioning, individual coaching gives you direct feedback and accountability.

Session Blueprint

EXAMPLE 60-MINUTE 1-ON-1 SESSION STRUCTURE

Here is an illustrative overview of how a private coaching appointment is structured.

Step 1 • 10 Mins

Check-in & Prep

Discuss recent recovery, soreness, or fatigue. Perform customized joint mobilization and neuromuscular activation drills.

Step 2 • 25 Mins

Primary Strength

Focused work on fundamental compound movement patterns (e.g. trap-bar deadlifts or dumbbell overhead pressing) with direct cueing.

Step 3 • 15 Mins

Accessory & Core

Unilateral exercises, anti-rotational core work, and posterior chain accessories to balance muscular symmetry.

Step 4 • 10 Mins

Guided Stretch & Review

Assisted stretching, breathing down-regulation, and discussion of takeaways and goals for the next appointment.
